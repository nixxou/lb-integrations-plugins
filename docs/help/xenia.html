In short

Xenia is an Xbox 360 emulator. The plugin installs and updates Xenia Canary (the actively developed version), sets it up so a game can start straight away, and takes care of the things Xenia does not do on its own:

  • it plays your games even when they are zipped (Xenia cannot open an archive),
  • it finds each game's title updates and DLC in your folders and puts them in place for that game only,
  • it gives every game its own options, and shows how well the game runs according to Xenia's compatibility list.

Getting started

Installing Xenia

In LaunchBox: Tools > Manage > Emulators > Add, pick Nixx-Xenia and let it download. On a first install the plugin also:

  • creates a profile named after your Windows account, and makes Xenia sign into it on start - without one, Xenia asks for a profile every time and games cannot save;
  • sets the console's language, country, time zone and clock the way your Windows is set. The language is the one games use: a game that has French will start in French.

A notification then tells you what was used: click Yes if it suits you, or Change... to correct it. An update of Xenia never touches your profile or console settings.

Already had Xenia without a profile? Open Tools > Nixx Integration Plugins Configuration..., the Xenia tab: the gamertag is filled in, click OK and the profile is created.

Importing games

Use LaunchBox's Import > ROM Files with the platform Microsoft Xbox 360. The plugin reads every file it is shown and cleans the list before you click Finish:

  • games stay - disc images (.iso), extracted discs, Xbox Live Arcade and Games on Demand packages, loose or zipped (for example the No-Intro digital sets);
  • title updates and DLC leave the list - they are not games. They are not lost: the plugin remembers them and offers them to their game;
  • files that are not Xbox 360 content leave the list, with the reason in the log.

Keep updates and DLC in your game folders (beside the games or in a subfolder): that is where the plugin looks for them.

Playing a game

When you click Play, the plugin:

  1. looks at the game's folder for anything new - the first time on a big folder this can take a little while, a window shows the progress; afterwards it is almost instant;
  2. if the game is zipped, unpacks it together with its chosen update and DLC;
  3. puts the chosen update and DLC where Xenia expects them, for this game only;
  4. starts Xenia with the game's options.

Where unpacked content goes

  • If everything fits under a size you choose (2 GB by default), it goes to a RAM disk for the session: nothing is written to your drive, and it is gone when you quit the game.
  • Otherwise it is unpacked once into a content folder on your drive (by default Emulators\Nixx-Xenia\lbip-content) and reused at the next launches. Changing which DLC are on does not unpack anything again.
  • You can give that folder a size limit: when it is full, the games you launched longest ago lose their unpacked content (all of it, never half), and get it back the next time you play them. A game marked Keep it on the disk (its Session tab) is never removed.
  • Changing a game's update or DLC later unpacks nothing again: what was unpacked stays, and only what is ticked is used. The Session tab can free the rest.

Your own files - the zips, the ISOs - are never changed or deleted.

The RAM disk needs a driver - Arsenal Image Mounter or ImDisk, both in the pack's installer - and the small helper the pack installs (the same one LiteBox uses). Without them, everything simply goes to the drive.

Options

For every game: the Xenia tab

Tools > Nixx Integration Plugins Configuration..., tab Xenia:

  • Profile: your gamertag (rename it, or create the profile).
  • Console: language, country, time zone, 24-hour clock, video region, console resolution, music volume, and a button to take them from Windows. These can only be changed while Xenia is closed.
  • Title updates and DLC: the content folder, its size limit, the RAM disk and its threshold, and whether the import list is cleaned.
  • Options for every game: graphics backend, rendering resolution (1x to 3x), VSync, frame-rate limit, gamma, anti-aliasing, sharpening (CAS / FSR), dithering, aspect ratio, vibration, mute, audio backend.

For one game: its right-click menu

Right-click a game > Nixx-Xenia : Options... opens three tabs:

  • Options - the same list, for this game only. Left unset, an option follows the Xenia tab, then Xenia's own setting.
  • Updates & DLC - choose the title update (one, or None for the game as released) and tick the DLC you want. By default: the most recent update and every DLC. Updates made for another version of the game (another region, for instance) are shown greyed out and cannot be picked.
  • Session - what the next launch will unpack for this game (the game itself when it is zipped, its update, its DLC), how big it is, and whether it goes to the RAM disk or the drive, and why. Here you can also force always the RAM disk or always the drive, tick Keep it on the disk so the size limit never removes it (and does not count it), and free what was unpacked once and is no longer chosen.
  • Compatibility - how the game runs according to Xenia's community list (Playable, Gameplay, Loads, Unplayable), its known problems, and a link to its report. The list is refreshed automatically when it is old.
Options are given to Xenia when the game starts and are never saved into Xenia's own configuration: a game's setting cannot leak into another game, or into Xenia opened on its own.

Saves

Each game's saves are visible in LaunchBox's save management: backups are made when you quit a game, and you can restore an older one from LaunchBox. The default name of a backup says which title update the game was running, for example MYSAVE [Title Update 3].

Saves belong to your Xenia profile - another reason the plugin creates one for you.

Limits and things to know

  • How well a game runs is Xenia's business, not the plugin's: check the Compatibility tab before blaming your setup.
  • Xenia applies one title update per game. Updates contain the earlier ones, so the most recent is usually right; pick an older one or None if a recent update breaks the game on Xenia.
  • Updates and DLC are looked for in the folders you imported from and in the game's own folder. Put them somewhere else and the game will not see them until those folders are scanned.
  • A disc image inside a zip is not read - keep ISOs unzipped.
  • Profile and console settings are written to Xenia's own files, so they can only be changed while Xenia is closed.
  • If you installed DLC yourself with Xenia's "Install Content", the plugin leaves that game's installed content alone.
  • On the RAM disk, the unpacked content of a session disappears when the game ends - that is the point. Your saves are elsewhere and are kept.

If something goes wrong

  • The game does not start: look at Xenia's own log, Emulators\Nixx-Xenia\xenia.log, and the Compatibility tab.
  • No updates or DLC in the game's tab: click Look at the folder again; check the files are in the game's folder or in a folder you imported from.
  • A profile prompt on start: open the Xenia tab of the configuration window and click OK.
  • The plugin's log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\lb-integrations-plugins\xenia.log (General tab: "Open the logs folder").
